A structured Gauss–Markov covariance for the Laplace approximation

Evaluating the Laplace Covariance Model (LCM) — A = diag(d) + diag(w)·K(a)·diag(w), a Matérn-½ chain on a learned 1-D ordering of the weights — as a hessian_structure="lcm" posterior, against diagonal, KFAC, and full Laplace across fidelity, predictive UQ, and cost.

Posterior fidelity Clear win vs Diag

1.6–2.9× lower KL to the exact posterior than diagonal Laplace; behind KFAC.

Cost & memory Win

3P storage vs P²; exact log-det where the dense posterior OOMs.

Predictive UQ Does not transfer

Ties tuned diagonal on regression; slightly worse on CIFAR-10. KFAC/Full lead.

Mechanism Explained

A single 1-D chain can't embed generic weight-space curvature — but recovers it exactly when it is chain-like.

One-paragraph summary. LCM is a strict, near-diagonal-cost improvement over diagonal Laplace in posterior fidelity, it recovers chain-structured curvature essentially perfectly, and it is memory-cheap and feasible where a dense posterior is not. But this fidelity gain does not reliably transfer to predictive uncertainty for weight-space Laplace: LCM ties a marginal-likelihood-tuned diagonal on regression, saturates with it on MNIST, and is slightly worse on CIFAR-10, while KFAC and full Laplace dominate. The ablations locate the cause precisely — one 1-D Markov chain cannot embed generic weight curvature (real-GGN residual ≈ 0.66), even though it captures curvature perfectly when the correlation is chain-like (residual ≈ 1e-4). LCM is best read as the strongest diagonal-cost, architecture-agnostic structured covariance — not a replacement for KFAC.

Baselines & protocol

All methods share the same MAP network and GGN backend, and differ only in the covariance structure. Each method tunes its own prior precision (and, for regression, its observation noise) by maximizing its own Laplace marginal likelihood — a fair, per-method best case. MAP (no weight uncertainty), Diag (diagonal), KFAC (Kronecker-factored), Full (exact dense reference), and LCM (ours, 3P params). Predictives are linearized (GLM); classification uses the probit link.

Synthesis

What the experiments establish

  • Fidelity (positive). A strict, near-diagonal-cost improvement over diagonal Laplace: 1.6–2.9× lower KL to the exact posterior, and near-perfect recovery of chain-structured curvature.
  • Cost (positive). 3P storage and an exact log-determinant that stays computable where the dense posterior OOMs.
  • Predictive UQ (neutral-to-negative). The fidelity gain does not carry to predictive uncertainty: LCM ties tuned diagonal on UCI, saturates on MNIST, is slightly worse on CIFAR-10. KFAC/Full dominate.
  • Mechanism (explanatory). A single 1-D chain cannot embed generic weight curvature — the a-priori limitation of a bandwidth-1 structure, confirmed empirically.

Practical guidance. Prefer LCM over diagonal for a cheap, architecture-agnostic structured covariance, when memory forbids denser structures, or when curvature is plausibly locally correlated — it never does worse than diagonal in fidelity and is far more robust. Prefer KFAC / full when the model has exploitable layer structure and predictive calibration is the target. The clearest next step, directly motivated by KFAC's edge, is a per-layer LCM chain (block-diagonal over layers).
